About the Institute

Empowering Hisar with Industry-Relevant Digital Education

HARTRON (Haryana Govt. Undertaking) Advanced Skill Centre at Hetram Park Colony, Hisar is focused on practical learning, digital literacy, and career development. Privacy Policy & Admission Terms

1. Information Collection & Privacy

HARTRON Advance Skill Centre collects student names, contact numbers, qualification details, and course preferences submitted through our enquiry forms strictly for academic counselling, batch scheduling, and student verification. We do not sell or share student data with third-party telemarketers.

2. Authentic Lab Training Standards

Our institute guarantees 1 student per computer workstation during practical class timings. Students are expected to adhere to lab rules, treat equipment with care, and maintain good attendance (minimum 75%) to qualify for periodic evaluations.

3. Course Enrolment & Certification

Course enrolment includes classroom lectures, lab access, and periodic skill assessments. Certificates of completion are awarded to learners who successfully pass the course evaluation and practical typing/software project submission.

4. Institutional Clarification

HARTRON Advance Skill Centre operates as a dedicated technical skill and computer education training institute in Haryana. We maintain complete transparency with students and parents regarding all syllabus details, batch durations, and educational services.
